Social Values

Social Values Statement

We, the Elders of Salem New Beginnings Church, believe that all people are created by God for His glory and find their greatest fulfillment in bringing Him glory. We, as leaders of this church, are committed to guiding our congregation according to the truth of God's Word. Most importantly, we are devoted to the Gospel of Jesus Christ, which offers purification from sin, forgiveness for all, and transformation of life into one of beauty, peace, and eternal life.

We acknowledge that many social issues today are complex and challenging. In this broken world, the allure of sin and its deceptive promises can easily lead people astray. We understand that some members of our church family may carry burdens from past choices. We firmly believe that Jesus Christ offers forgiveness to all who acknowledge and turn from their sin.

However, we also strongly emphasize the importance of resisting temptation in the first place. While Salem New Beginnings Church strives to have open discussions, we wish to articulate our convictions on various social issues clearly. The Scriptural support for each section should be understood within its broader context and as a representative sample of the extensive biblical material that undergirds our beliefs.

The Love of God

God's profound love for all of creation, especially for humanity, motivated Him to offer redeeming grace through Jesus Christ. Regardless of their past, we believe that anyone can experience forgiveness and be welcomed into God's eternal family through sincere repentance and faith in Jesus Christ. Psalm 86:15; 136:26; John 3:16-18; Ephesians 2:4-7; 1 John 3:1

Creation

We believe God created the world, distinct from Himself, and designed it to reflect His glory, even in its fallen state. Humanity was created in God's image to rule and care for the earth. As God's new creation, believers are called to steward the environment in a way that points to God's redemptive plan for a renewed heaven and earth. Until then, we are responsible to God for caring for creation in a way that honors Him and benefits our neighbors. Psalm 19:1; Genesis 1:26, 27; 2:15; James 3:8, 9; 2 Corinthians 5:17-21; Romans 8:18-23; 2 Peter 3:11-13

Humanity

We believe mankind descended from Adam and Eve who were specially and uniquely created and intentionally designed as male and female to bear his image as the crowning work of creation. As such, all human life is sacred and worthy of respect and Christian love. Genesis 1:26-28; James 3:8, 9; Psalm 104:10-15, 27-31; Genesis 9:5, 6; Exodus 20:13; Psalm 8

Sanctity of Life

We believe human life begins at the moment of conception and continues until God’s predetermined number of days for that life are completed. Consequently, we are opposed to in vitro and in vivo abortion, and all forms of euthanasia. Genesis 2:7; Deuteronomy 30:19; Psalm 139:13-16; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 7:14; 8:8; 1 Corinthians 15:26, 54-56; Hebrews 2:9, 14, 15; Revelation 21:4

Gender

We believe that God created human beings as male and female, and we oppose intentional actions, medical interventions, or others that aim to alter or contradict this inherent design. Genesis 1:26-28, 31; 2:25; Deuteronomy 22:5; 1 Corinthians 6:9, 10

Marriage

We believe that God, as the Creator, defines marriage. According to Genesis 2:24, marriage is a union between one man and one woman, forming a new family unit that takes priority over other relationships. Marriage involves a public commitment and the physical consummation of the union. We believe that only God has the authority to define marriage and that same-sex relationships do not fulfill the biblical definition of marriage. Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:4-9; Romans 1:21-32; 1 Corinthians 6:9-11; 7:1-4; 1 Timothy 1:8-11

Human Sexuality

We believe that human sexuality is a God-given gift designed to be expressed within the context of marriage between one man and one woman. Sexual activity outside of this biblical definition of marriage is not by God's will. Pornography, in all its forms, including literature, photography, art, movies, and music, is harmful and should be avoided by all believers. Genesis 4:1; Matthew 15:19; 1 Corinthians 6:15-18; 7:1-4; Hebrews 13:4; Matthew 5:27, 28

Race and Imago Dei

We believe God created all humanity from Adam and Eve, making all people equal in God's sight. The concept of 'race,' as commonly understood, is a social construct often used to justify discrimination based on skin color or other superficial characteristics. The Bible, however, teaches there is only one race, the human race, and that all people are blood relatives descended from Adam and Eve. The Bible emphasizes the importance of loving our neighbor as ourselves. The idea of inherent racial superiority is a harmful and unfounded social construct. It has no basis in scientific fact and has been used to justify oppression and inequality throughout history. The Bible teaches that all humans are created in the image of God and are, therefore, equal in His sight. We are called to love and respect all people, regardless of their background or appearance. Acts 17:26, 27; Genesis 1:26-28; Genesis 10; Matthew 19:19; Luke 6:27

Justice

We believe that true justice originates with God, who is perfectly just. Human justice should reflect God's character and align with His laws, as Scripture reveals. As God's image-bearers, all people are accountable to Him for living justly in all aspects of life. However, due to sin, human beings are inherently unjust. As those who have been made right with God through Christ, Christians are called to pursue true justice. Their lives should reflect the values and character of their King. Any pursuit of justice must be consistent with God's nature and His laws. Ultimately, final and perfect justice will be administered by God when all people stand before Him for judgment. Those who have been justified through faith in Christ will receive eternal life, while those who have rejected God's law will face eternal condemnation. Genesis 1:26, 27; Isaiah 51:4; 61:8; Micah 6:8; Matthew 25:31-46; Revelation 20:11-15; Galatians 3:28

Common Grace and Law

We believe that human sin would lead to societal chaos without God's restraining grace. God, in His kindness, restrains sin through common grace, particularly through civil government and the rule of law. Those who create and enforce laws represent God's authority and are accountable to Him for administering justice. They deserve respect and honor, even when they make mistakes. Citizens have a responsibility to obey just laws as a contribution to a peaceful and orderly society and as an act of obedience to God, the ultimate Lawgiver. We affirm that ultimate allegiance belongs to God. When human laws conflict with God's commands, our obedience to God must take precedence. Exodus 20:2-17; Psalm 19:7-14; Isaiah 33:22; 42:4; 51:4; Matthew 5:17-19; Romans 13:1-7; Titus 3:1; Acts 4: 19-20; Acts 5:29; 1 Peter 2:13-17; Revelation 13:1-10; 19:11-16
