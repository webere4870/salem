Statement of Faith

Preface

Salem New Beginnings Church exists to make and multiply believers who magnify the glory of God. While we are engaged in different ministries in our attempt to accomplish this mission, our primary task is to teach and live God’s Word in the power of the Spirit, engaging our world as salt and light. The Leadership of Salem New Beginnings Church is deeply committed to studying and teaching God’s Word with diligence, clarity, authority, and love. We also strive to live it consistently and expect to be held accountable. As a community of believers, we desire to extend fellowship to all who have accepted Jesus as Lord and Savior. We request that those who worship with us respect our doctrinal convictions and actively seek to preserve the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace by His grace and for His glory.

Introduction

The What We Teach sections present the basic teaching position of the Elders of Salem New Beginnings Church. This section expands on the Statement of Faith affirmed by those who desire to partner with us through membership.

The Statement of Faith presents the core, shared, doctrinal beliefs that form the foundation of our unity as a church family. These are the truths we all affirm. To be a member, you must only affirm the Statement of Faith and complete the other steps in the membership process. However, as Elders, we want you to know where we stand on issues beyond our Statement of Faith. The What We Teach will do just that. In each section, the Statement of Faith is followed by additional material describing our teaching position. We intend to set our stance on the topics and allow for profitable theological discussions at Salem New Beginnings. We realize godly Christians have debated doctrinal issues for centuries, and too often, the dialogue has been unedifying and divisive. We hope that here at Salem New Beginnings Church, discussions about God’s truth will be covered in God’s love and that God’s Word will be the final arbiter of what we believe and teach.

Our Church exists to make and multiply believers who magnify the glory of God. While we are engaged in different ministries in our attempt to accomplish this mission, our primary task is to teach and live God’s Word in the power of the Spirit, engaging our world as salt and light.

God
EFCA

We believe in one God, Creator of all things, holy, infinitely perfect, and eternally existing in a loving unity of three equally divine Persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. Having limitless knowledge and sovereign power, God has graciously purposed from eternity to redeem a people for Himself and to make all things new for His own glory


What We Teach About God

God the Father is the sole, absolute Ruler of the universe, sustaining all things by His power. He has eternally decreed all that comes to pass, guiding and directing all things towards His ultimate plan and glory. While sovereign, God is not the author of sin and holds all moral beings accountable for their choices. We teach that God saves all who turn to Christ in repentance and faith from sin and wrath, adopting them into His family as beloved children. God graciously chose those whom He would have as His own from eternity, electing them to salvation through the work of the Holy Spirit and the Gospel, bringing them to repentance and faith.

The Bible
EFCA

We believe that God has spoken in the Scriptures, both Old and New Testaments, through the words of human authors. As the verbally inspired Word of God, the Bible is without error in the original writings, the complete revelation of His will for salvation, and the ultimate authority by which every realm of human knowledge and endeavor should be judged. Therefore, it is to be believed in all that it teaches, obeyed in all that it requires, and trusted in all that it promises.


What We Teach About the Bible

We believe and teach the Bible, the word of God, is without error in the original transmission. Human authors, guided by the Spirit, wrote Scripture in their own words while accurately conveying God's message. We believe and teach that God has preserved His Word throughout history, and today, through careful textual criticism, we have the original words God intended. We believe and teach that the Bible is free from contradictions when understood within its original context and language. We believe that diligent study, informed by the Holy Spirit, will ultimately vindicate the Bible's inerrancy, infallibility, and full authority. We affirm the importance of accurate translations that faithfully convey both the original authors' words and intended meaning. We believe in the necessity of studying the Bible with diligence and integrity. We believe the primary task of interpretation is to understand the original author's intended meaning for their original audience. From this understanding, we can then apply the timeless truths of Scripture to our own lives and the lives of others. While there may be various applications of Scripture, we believe there is only one true interpretation, discoverable through careful historical, grammatical, and theological study. The Bible is the eternal and inerrant Word of God, the greatest treasure of knowledge, and the foundation of our teaching and preaching.

The Human Condition
EFCA

We believe that God created Adam and Eve in His image, but they sinned when tempted by Satan. In union with Adam, human beings are sinners by nature and by choice, alienated from God, and under His wrath. Only through God’s saving work in Jesus Christ can we be rescued, reconciled and renewed.


What We Teach About the Human Condition

We believe and teach God created mankind directly and without sin. However, Adam and Eve yielded to Satan's temptation and disobeyed God, introducing sin into the world and passing on both their guilt and sinful nature to all their posterity. We believe and teach despite the presence of sin, humanity continues to experience God's common grace. As God's image-bearers, human life is sacred and valuable, demanding protection and respect. We affirm that love for God and our neighbors should characterize Christian living. However, given the fallen state of humanity, true and lasting peace can only be found through the transformative power of the Gospel of Jesus Christ and the ultimate establishment of the Kingdom of God on earth.

Jesus Christ
EFCA

We believe that Jesus Christ is God incarnate, truly God and truly man, one Person in two natures. Jesus—Israel's promised Messiah—was conceived through the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary. He lived a sinless life, was crucified under Pontius Pilate, arose bodily from the dead, ascended into heaven and sits at the right hand of God the Father as our High Priest and Advocate.


What We Teach About the Jesus Christ

We believe and teach that God the Father created all things through God the Son, the second Person of the Trinity, and that through God the Son all things continue to hold together and operate. That, in the incarnation, the eternally existing second Person of the Trinity added all the essential characteristics of humanity becoming the God-man. He took on the humble position of a slave while never divesting Himself of any divine attributes. While Jesus Christ was fully human, He did not inherit a sinful nature, being born of a virgin. The purpose then of the incarnation was to reveal God to man, resolve the problem of sin, and rule over the Kingdom of God. We believe and teach that Jesus Christ suffered and died on the cross, the just for the unjust, that He might rescue us from the domain of darkness and bring us to God. Jesus’ resurrection was real, bodily, and a complete triumph over sin and death, and when He returns, believers will return with Him in glory. Jesus Christ is the Head of His church and continues to exercise his offices of Prophet, Priest, and King through godly human leadership. Jesus Christ is now seated at the Father's right hand above and one day will return in glory and honor to rescue the righteous and judge the world.

The Work of Christ
EFCA

We believe that Jesus Christ, as our representative and substitute, shed His blood on the cross as the perfect, all-sufficient sacrifice for our sins. His atoning death and victorious resurrection constitute the only ground for salvation.


What We Teach About the Work of Christ

That salvation is solely of God's grace, not earned through human merit. That God, in His sovereign choice, has elected certain individuals in Christ from among fallen humanity for salvation. This election includes regeneration, justification, sanctification, and ultimate glorification. That God has ordained not only the ends of salvation but also the means whereby sinners are brought out of darkness and into light. It is through the Gospel, lived out and proclaimed by believers, that God the Spirit brings sinners to repentance and faith and secures their eternal redemption. Sovereign election does not negate individual responsibility to repent and believe in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior. God's choice for salvation is not based on any foreseen human action or merit but solely on His grace and mercy. God's saving work is consistent with all His attributes, including omniscience, justice, holiness, wisdom, grace, and love. Jesus Christ, through His sacrificial death on the cross, accomplished our salvation. His death was voluntary, vicarious (substituting for us), propitiatory (appeasing God's wrath), and redemptive. Through the atoning death of Jesus Christ, believing sinners are freed from the punishment, penalty, and power of sin. They are declared righteous (justified) by God, receive eternal life, and are adopted into God's family as beloved children. On the cross, our sins were imputed to Christ, and He fully paid the penalty for them. By faith, we are credited with the righteousness of Christ, accepted as righteous in His sight. The resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead confirms our justification and guarantees the future resurrection of all believers. Every believer is a saint, indwelt by the Holy Spirit, and fully accepted and forgiven by God. This position before God is secure upon salvation. Despite justification, believers still struggle with indwelling sin. They are called to persevere in holiness, resisting temptation through the power of the Holy Spirit. This ongoing process is known as sanctification. All believers, indwelt by the Holy Spirit, will persevere in holiness until they see Jesus face-to-face. This security rests ultimately on the perseverance of God Himself, who works within believers to will and to do His good pleasure.

The Holy Spirit
EFCA

We believe that the Holy Spirit, in all that He does, glorifies the Lord Jesus Christ. He convicts the world of its guilt. He regenerates sinners, and in Him they are baptized into union with Christ and adopted as heirs in the family of God. He also indwells, illuminates, guides, equips and empowers believers for Christ-like living and service.


What We Teach About the Holy Spirit

We affirm and teach that the Holy Spirit is a divine Person, eternal and possessing all the attributes of deity. He is active in creation, the incarnation, and the inspiration of Scripture. The Spirit regenerates sinners, granting them repentance and faith through the Gospel. He illuminates believers' minds, empowers them for holy living, and sovereignly distributes spiritual gifts. We believe and teach that the gifts of tongues and other sign gifts in the early days of the church were to authenticate the apostles as revealers of divine truth and were never intended to be characteristic of the lives of believers today. God continues to work miracles, effect miraculous healing, and demonstrate His power as our universe's sovereign creator and sustainer. Every believer should pray for healing, believing God may act in ways that confound the medical and scientific community. We believe and teach that God, while active in this way, no longer grants miraculous power to individuals.

The Church
EFCA

We believe that the true church comprises all who have been justified by God's grace through faith alone in Christ alone. They are united by the Holy Spirit in the body of Christ, of which He is the Head. The true church is manifest in local churches, whose membership should be composed only of believers. The Lord Jesus mandated two ordinances, baptism and the Lord’s Supper, which visibly and tangibly express the gospel. Though they are not the means of salvation, when celebrated by the church in genuine faith, these ordinances confirm and nourish the believer.


What We Teach About the Church

Jesus Christ is the head of the church. He continues to govern His church through godly and gifted Elders, set apart by the Holy Spirit and affirmed by the congregation. These Elders, serving as a plurality, are accountable to God and the church to fulfill the biblical qualifications of their office. Baptism is a voluntary act of public testimony for regenerate believers who testify of their salvation. Through immersion, they identify with Christ's death, burial, and resurrection and unite with the local church. Baptism is a community-forming event and should be celebrated during corporate worship. The Lord's Supper is a communal meal where believers, in faith, acknowledge Christ as essential for their spiritual nourishment. While the bread and cup remain physical elements, they serve as reminders of Christ's atoning work. Jesus Christ has commissioned the church to go into all the world, making and multiplying disciples to the glory of God.

Christian Living
EFCA

We believe that God's justifying grace must not be separated from His sanctifying power and purpose. God commands us to love Him supremely and others sacrificially, and to live out our faith with care for one another, compassion toward the poor and justice for the oppressed. With God’s Word, the Spirit’s power, and fervent prayer in Christ’s name, we are to combat the spiritual forces of evil. In obedience to Christ’s commission, we are to make disciples among all people, always bearing witness to the gospel in word and deed.


What We Teach About Christian Living

Salvation is the supernatural work of God in a person that manifests itself in a transformed life. True believers will demonstrate this transformation through the fruit of the Spirit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. This fruit includes a hunger for God's Word, a pursuit of righteousness, increased godliness and Christ-likeness, a desire for fellowship with other believers, and a commitment to obey God in all things. True faith is always accompanied by repentance from sin. Repentance is agreeing with God that you are sinful, confessing your sins to Him, and consciously choosing to turn from sin and pursue Christ and obedience to Him. It isn’t enough to believe certain facts about Christ. Even Satan and his demons believe in the true God, but they don’t love and obey Him. Saving faith is always accompanied by an increasing pattern of obedience.

Christ's Return
EFCA

We believe in the personal, bodily and glorious return of our Lord Jesus Christ. The coming of Christ, at a time known only to God, demands constant expectancy and, as our blessed hope, motivates the believer to godly living, sacrificial service and energetic mission.


What We Teach About Christ's Return

Jesus Christ will return to earth before the Millennium, establishing a reign of righteousness. This blessed hope motivates Christ-followers to live with expectancy, diligence, and obedience in this sinful world. All people will experience resurrection. Believers in Jesus Christ will be resurrected to eternal life in the new heavens and new earth, while those who have died without faith will face eternal separation from God in hell. Following the final judgment, Jesus Christ will deliver the kingdom back to God the Father, fulfilling the eternal plan of redemption and affirming God's eternal reign.

Response and Eternal Destiny
EFCA

We believe that God commands everyone everywhere to believe the gospel by turning to Him in repentance and receiving the Lord Jesus Christ. We believe that God will raise the dead bodily and judge the world, assigning the unbeliever to condemnation and eternal conscious punishment and the believer to eternal blessedness and joy with the Lord in the new heaven and the new earth, to the praise of His glorious grace. Amen.


What We Teach About Eternal Destiny

Mankind faces two eternal destinies: heaven or hell. All people will die once and face judgment. Unbelievers will stand before the Great White Throne to be judged according to their deeds. Those who have repented of their sins and trusted in Jesus Christ will experience eternal happiness in heaven, receiving rewards for their faithful service. Those who reject the Gospel and refuse to submit to Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior will suffer conscious, eternal torment in hell, separated from the presence of God and His angels.
