Plan Your Visit

Welcome to Salem! If you'd like to “Plan Your Visit” with us, just fill out the form below to get started! Your information will then be sent to a member of our Connections Team, who will meet you at the Welcome Center in the lobby. If you have young children under the age of six attending Sunday School, they will need to be checked in, and volunteers will be available to assist you. We look forward to meeting you! Please submit this form no later than 7am on the Sunday you plan to visit.

We sing a mixture of hymns and praise songs, old and new, during Sunday services. Music is a wonderful gift from God, enabling us to combine doctrine and devotion to glorify God. These songs direct our affections to worship our Triune God, helping us remember our need for our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ.

We preach through the Bible verse by verse, chapter by chapter, book by book. The Bible holds the very words of God and is profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness. This is why it’s at the core of our teaching. Check out what we believe

We desire people to know and glorify God. We are called to be gospel people, faithfully speaking and living the Good News to our neighbors, community, and the world. We trust God to make and multiply believers for His glory. See if you are going to heaven.
